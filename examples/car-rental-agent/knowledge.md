# Northwind Car Rentals: approved plans and rules

All figures below are made up for this example. Replace them with your own before taking real calls.

## Cities
Northwind operates in Pune and Bengaluru only.

## Eligibility
- A valid four-wheeler driving licence in the driver's own name, at least 1 year old.
- Age 21 to 55.
- New drivers are welcome on the weekly rental plan.
- A blocked Uber ID is not eligible (record ISUID).

## Weekly rental
- Pune: refundable security deposit ₹5,000; rent ₹4,500 per week.
- Bengaluru: refundable security deposit ₹6,000; rent ₹5,000 per week.
- Regular servicing and insurance are included. Fuel and tolls are paid by the driver.

## Salaried driver
- Needs at least 2 years of ride-hailing experience and an Uber rating of 4.7 or more.
- Fixed salary ₹22,000 per month plus trip incentives; no security deposit.
- Only in Bengaluru.

## Plan routing
- Meets the salaried-driver criteria and lives in Bengaluru: offer the salaried plan first.
- Everyone else who is eligible: offer the weekly rental.

## Current offer
- First week's rent is half price for drivers who join this month. No other discounts exist.

## Interest rules
- Interested only if the driver agrees to a telecaller follow-up or asks for the next joining step.
- "I'll think about it" with a follow-up agreed counts as INTERESTED with interest_strength tentative.
